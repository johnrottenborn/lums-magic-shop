# Story and lore

The designer owns the story. Talk new story content through with them before building it.

## Setting and tone

At the edge of the world sits a tiny magic shop. A hermit seeking peace finds herself sharing a quiet life with an unexpected apprentice.
The game should feel soft, whimsical and enchanting, like a children's storybook: detailed, living environments (wisps, sprites, swaying grass and bushes) and no pixel art.

## Prologue (final, don't change it)

- **Cosmic**, Lum's blue-haired apprentice, is sent to deal with slimes that are eating Lum's magic shrubs. He can't cast spells. He fights with the powders and potions he finds.
- A shadowy voice gives him the **Ancient Blade** (+50 attack). The voice tells him Lum will never teach him real magic, and he starts to doubt her. He hides the blade from Lum.
- At night the voice tells him to cut a path out of the garden. He reaches the cemetery, slays skeletons, and the seal breaks. He is possessed and becomes the **Demon God, Fokalur**, and the world quakes.
- Before Cosmic has the Ancient Blade, talking to Lum gets: "Use your imagination, slimes could be hiding anywhere!"

## Lore

- Lum's real name is **Lumia**, one of the four heroes of legend who sealed the Demon God a thousand years ago. Making the seal made them immortal. Lumia made mirror images of herself to live many lives, and Lum is one of them.
- **Lumi**, Cosmic's mother, is another mirror image (she looks very much like Lum). She is mayor of a sea town across Stranglethorn Forest. She sent Cosmic to watch over Lum, partly to protect him, because she had heard a faint shadowy voice.
- **Spar** is a hero of legend: the wielder of fire and the most powerful sword user. He is reborn like a phoenix, but only by dying in fire.
- **Gram** is a hero of legend: an immortal king living one single life. His element is earth.
- Main arc: Lum must find and reseal the Demon God. She regains her power by reuniting with all of Lumia's mirror images, then defeats him alongside the other heroes.

## Chapter 1 (in progress, name not decided)

- Lum is now the hero. She wakes, can't find Cosmic, sees the hedge cut by a powerful blade in novice hands, and follows muddy boot prints into the **Whispering Woods**. The well and pond are closed off. The garden and shed have no enemies.
- **Vane**, the gravekeeper, never fights. A **Grave Reaper** charges his locked door; beating it unlocks the door. Vane tells of the storm, last night's events and a blue-haired boy. He gives Lum a **lantern** and, one time only, two healing potions.
- With the lantern, Vane remembers an old story about travellers following flowers through the maze. He admits it's not much help and promises to keep a lookout for the boy.
- **Mixing**: near a fire, a mortar-and-pestle emote lets Lum turn parts into powders and potions. Only Lum can mix.
- **Stranglethorn Forest** is one big bramble maze, reached from the east of the Whispering Woods.
  - Without the lantern, Lum only sees the edge of the forest, and her lines say she needs a better light.
  - The true path is marked by **red roses**, grown from seeds dropped by travellers who once crossed it. Few have entered recently.
  - Enemies: spiders, Flora, red slimes and red foxes. The boss is the **Broodmother**, a large spider.
  - Planned, not built yet: the Demon God taunts Lum by appearing as Cosmic and fading, with an eerie demonic call.
- **Jak**, a lost traveller with a sack and an axe, rests at a dead end near the start of Stranglethorn at 5/20 HP. A winged demon engulfed his swordsman friend and took his light. When Lum heals him with a potion or powder, he shares the seed lore and joins. His combo is **Staff Bolt**.
- Later in Chapter 1, Lum sees burning trees, rescues a toddler who becomes the boy **Spar**, and ends the chapter by meeting **Lumi** to tell her Cosmic is missing.
